# SEIRA — Research Goal

Research the international state of the art supporting the SEIRA project.

Complete exactly 5 tasks:

1. Crop phenology extraction and modelling from Sentinel-2 time series.

2. Crop anomaly and stress detection from EO time series, especially methods comparing observed crop trajectories with expected, historical, or reference phenological trajectories.

3. Explainable AI for agricultural EO: methods that provide interpretable evidence for detected crop anomalies, stress, or risk.

4. Vision-language models and LLMs for EO/agriculture, especially methods generating human-readable descriptions, explanations, or risk narratives from satellite data.

5. Existing commercial crop-monitoring platforms and research systems combining EO time series, phenology, anomaly/risk detection, explainability, and human-readable outputs. Identify the remaining gap relevant to SEIRA.

For each task:
- identify the strongest and most relevant references;
- prioritize 2021–2026 peer-reviewed papers and primary sources;
- verify every reference and DOI/URL;
- summarize the main methods and results;
- clearly identify what is still missing compared with SEIRA.

Do not write the SEIRA proposal.
Do not invent references.
Produce a separate output for each of the 5 tasks.
